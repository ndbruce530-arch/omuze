2. What Omuze Is

Omuze helps members of the Otjiherero-speaking community record, preserve and explore their family lineage, including clan identity (eanda, omuna, uyana), and share family and community news. Family tree information is a shared, community-built record: people you add may also appear in the trees of relatives who use the App.
